Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

MyMedFriend is a student project. This page explains, in plain language, what the app and this website do with information. It is not legal advice.

The app

  • What it stores: your medications, schedules, pill counts, dose history, tags, and any notes you write.
  • Where it is stored: only in a database on your own phone.
  • Accounts: there are none. You do not give an email, a password, or a name.
  • Sending data: the app does not send your information to me or to any server. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled by your phone. They do not use a notification server.

When information leaves your phone

  • Exports: when you create a PDF or CSV and share it, the file goes where you send it. After that, protecting the file is up to you and whoever receives it.
  • Phone backups: your phone's own backup service, such as iCloud or Google backup, may copy the app's data depending on your phone's settings. That is controlled by your phone and its maker.
  • Encryption: the app does not encrypt its stored data. Use your phone's screen lock to protect it.

Deleting your data

  • Use "Delete everything" in the app. It erases all data in the app and cancels scheduled reminders.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone.
  • Files you exported, and any phone backups, are separate and are not removed by this. Delete them yourself.
  • There is no cloud copy. If you lose your phone and have not exported your records, they are gone.

This website

  • This site is a set of plain pages. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies set by me.
  • The demo on the home page runs in your browser with sample data. Nothing you type into it is saved or sent.
  • If you email me, I receive your email address and message. I use them only to reply, and I will not share them. Please do not send personal medical information.
  • My web host may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.

Children

The app collects nothing about anyone, including children, because it keeps no data off the phone. A parent or guardian should decide whether a child uses it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.
