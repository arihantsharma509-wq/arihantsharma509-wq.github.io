What this is

MyMedFriend is a student project. It helps you organize medication schedules and keep a self-reported log. It is not a medical device, it is not reviewed or approved by any regulator, and it does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice.

Medical decisions

  • Follow the instructions from your doctor, pharmacist, or care team. Your prescription label and their written instructions come first.
  • Do not change how you take any medicine because of something in this app.
  • For an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Reminders and counts can be wrong

  • Reminders depend on your phone's settings, such as battery saver and notification permissions. They can be delayed or missed. Do not use them as your only reminder.
  • Pill counts and low-stock alerts only reflect what you log. Check your real supply.
  • The app can contain mistakes. Check your records against your medicine and your care team's instructions.

Your data and backups

  • Your records live only on your phone. There is no cloud backup.
  • You are responsible for exporting your records if you want to keep them. If you lose your phone or delete the app, your records are gone.
  • Exports are not marked as clinical documents. They contain self-reported information, and a CSV export carries no disclaimer note.
